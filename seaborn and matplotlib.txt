Matplotlib::

Matplotlib is a fundamental plotting library in Python.
It allows you to create basic to advanced graphs such as:

.Line charts
.Bar charts
.Pie charts
.Histograms
.Scatter plots

Seaborn::

Seaborn is a higher-level statistical visualization library, built on top of Matplotlib.
It makes charts prettier, easier, and better for analysis.

>>Key Points
.Creates beautiful graphs with less code.
.Better for statistical plots, like:
.Heatmaps
.Pairplots
.Boxplots
.Violin plots
.Distribution plots
